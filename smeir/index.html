Social Media Environment and Internet Replication

Overview

My current work project. Social Media Environment and Internet Replication (SMEIR) replicates social media environments for training purposes. It consists of a controller application and individual sites mirroring common social media sites (like Twitter, Facebook).

My responsibilities for this system include designing and developing the front end, speaking with users on site to gather requirements.

Mockups/Wireframes cannot be provided.

The Synthetic Internet Training Environment (SITE) was an enormous part of BR22. It provides a "click-able, interactive" internet that dynamically replicates various websites and social media sites to allow Cyber, Information Warfare, and PAO and OSINT interaction/play for the training audience.
