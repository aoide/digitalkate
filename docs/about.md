# Designer. Coder. Geek.

## Kate. Defined.

Hi! I'm Kate.

I work as full stack designer with a focus on enterprise applications, particularly for the Department of Defense.

I've run the full gambit of the application creation process, so I understand the down wind impacts of a lot of what I do and design, as well as giving me the ability to talk to various stakeholders, internal and external.

My work has primarily revolved around crafting cohesive, scalable design systems and experiences for interconnected applications, whether across devices or within a larger suite.

I find joy in creating interfaces for applications utilizing large data sets and complex workflows.

## What does this mean for you?

I'm used to wearing multiple hats to get a job done.

I talk to users, write requirements, drive user flows, talk to users more, craft wire frames, create behavior specifications, more user conversations, and prototype everything.

I also implement front end interfaces using React, Angular, JQuery, HTML, and CSS/SCSS.

## As for the rest…

I'm owned by 2 cats, and I read... a lot. I love gaming and farmer's markets… and drinking chai. Lots of chai.
